IIAbout me

Counted right, specified right, lit beautifully.

I am a lighting estimation and technical specialist with 12+ years in lighting across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and India. My work starts where a project's drawings and tender documents land: reviewing scope, specifications and submission requirements, then building lighting BOQs, quantity take-offs, material price lists and bid package estimates that hold up to scrutiny.

On the technical side, I prepare and review lighting layouts, lux-level calculations in DIALux, fixture specifications, technical submittals and shop drawings in AutoCAD, and coordinate with subcontractors, suppliers and site teams through material inspection and supervision.

Design completes the picture: architectural, interior, facade and landscape lighting for hospitality and commercial projects, from fixture selection to visualisation.

XTender & estimation experience

From tender documents to a priced bid.

How a lighting package moves through estimation, step by step.

  1. i.

    Tender review

    Review project drawings and tender documents for scope, submission requirements and specifications.

  2. ii.

    Take-offs & BOQ

    Quantity and material take-offs, fixture schedules and lighting BOQs, priced from material price lists.

  3. iii.

    Supplier pricing

    Obtain pricing and scope of work from subcontractors and suppliers for competitive, cost-effective solutions.

  4. iv.

    Technical compliance

    Lighting calculations, fixture specifications and technical submittals aligned to the specification.

  5. v.

    Bid packages

    Prequalification submissions, RFQs and RFIs, and bid package estimates for forecasting and budget control.

  6. vi.

    Value engineering

    Alternative fixtures and cost optimisation without losing the design intent.

  7. vii.

    Cost control

    Compare conceptual estimates with actual costs, and coordinate progress payments.

  8. viii.

    Site & quality

    Material inspections and site supervision so specifications and quality standards are met.
